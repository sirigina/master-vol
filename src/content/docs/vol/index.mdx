Every lesson has a short ID like **C2** (module C, lesson 2). Follow them in order the first time; after that, jump straight to any ID to review. Lessons list what they build on at the top, so you always know what to revisit.

## How each lesson works

- **Two levels of explanation.** *Plain English* boxes use everyday analogies; *Pro detail* boxes give the math and how traders use it. Use the toggle at the top of any lesson to show one or both.
- **Real market numbers.** Examples use one frozen snapshot of SPY and its options (October 27, 2026 close), so numbers agree across every lesson.
- **Interactive figures.** Drag the sliders. Most charts answer "what if?" better than any paragraph.
- **A self-check at the end.** Your answers stay in your browser.
